# Third-party components

## Material Design In XAML Toolkit

`assets/Assembly/MaterialDesignThemes.Wpf.dll` and `assets/Assembly/MaterialDesignColors.dll` are unmodified builds of the [Material Design In XAML Toolkit](https://github.com/MaterialDesignInXAML/MaterialDesignInXamlToolkit), used for PSGUI's WPF styling.

## PS2EXE

Release executables are compiled with [PS2EXE](https://github.com/MScholtes/PS2EXE) (MIT License, Markus Scholtes). It is used at build time only and is not redistributed.
